Questions we get

Straight answers, the same ones we give on the first call.

Where is Picante Studio and who do you work with?

Delhi NCR, India, working with brands anywhere. Most of our work is for food and drink, D2C, jewellery and watch brands, and SaaS companies: QCSTech and QED Vault are named clients, and the concept films on the site show the consumer brands we are built for.

What is the minimum project?

INR 10,000, excluding GST. Below that, the free resources on this site will get you further than a quote would.

How do you charge?

Fixed prices with defined deliverables, revision rounds, timeline and usage, quoted from the rate card. Projects under INR 1 lakh are paid 50 percent on acceptance and 50 percent on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

How fast can you deliver?

Motion in 7 to 10 business days, design in 5 to 7, from an approved direction. Rush under four business days is plus 50 percent. Our fastest client film went from script to screen in one week.

Do you do photo or video shoots?

Not in-house. We build films and stills in 3D and motion, which is why there is no shoot to schedule. If a brief needs a camera crew, we say so on the first call and can bring in a partner at cost.

Do you work with brands outside Delhi?

Yes. Everything runs remotely: brief, direction page, drafts and delivery. Meetings in Delhi NCR when it helps.

How a project runs

Short, in writing, and the same every time.

  1. BriefYou send the product, the goal and the deadline. We come back with an approach and a quote.
  2. DirectionOne page: the idea, the references, the beat sheet or the site map. You approve it before anything is made.
  3. MakeFilms, systems and sites are built in-house, so the first draft is the real thing, not a mock-up.
  4. RefineRevision rounds are agreed up front. Feedback goes in one list, changes come back as one version.
  5. DeliverFinal files in every format you run, or a live site on its own address. Then, if you want it, a monthly flow.
