Who this is for

D2C, food and drink, jewellery and SaaS brands posting several times a week, with a marketing lead who is already full and no in-house designer. Also brands about to launch who need launch week, proof and reminder content ready before day one.

What you get

Scale 15: five statics, five carousels and five story sets a month. Scale 30: ten of each. Scale 45: fifteen of each. Add four motion posts a month, or an ad creative pack of eight static ad variants and two motion cutdowns. Every asset in the formats you run, delivered in batches with captions ready to schedule.

How it works

A social creative system comes first: templates, type and colour rules, so every post looks like one brand. If you already have one, we start from it. Then a monthly calendar with the mix agreed, batches delivered in 3 to 5 business days, one revision round per asset. Unused assets roll over one month, and the mix can be rebalanced within the same budget: two statics equal one carousel.

Launch offer

The first three retainer clients get launch terms in exchange for a published case study. Ask on the first call.

How a month runs

Short, in writing, and the same every time.

  2. CalendarThe month's mix is agreed up front: what goes out, when, in which formats.

Questions we get

Straight answers, the same ones we give on the first call.

What does monthly content cost?

It depends on the tier, whether a creative system exists, and whether motion or ad packs are added. Every retainer is quoted as a fixed monthly fee within a day of the brief, and runs 15 to 20 percent under the same assets bought one at a time.

Is there a minimum term?

Three months, billed monthly in advance. Unused assets roll over one month.

Do you write the captions?

Yes. Every batch comes with captions and a posting order, ready to schedule in your tool of choice. Posting and community replies stay with you unless agreed otherwise.

Do you need a brand system first?

Yes. A social creative system of six to ten templates with type and colour rules is what keeps a month of content looking like one brand. If you have one we use it; if not we build it in the first two weeks.

Can we change the mix month to month?

Yes, within the same budget. Two statics equal one carousel, and story sets can swap for statics. Tell us before the month's calendar is set.
