Product films

Films that make people want the product, built in motion so there is no shoot to schedule. For food and drink, D2C, jewellery and SaaS brands with a launch coming and nothing that shows the product yet.

Start a projectSee the prices

Who this is for

A launch is coming and there is no film. A shoot is not in the budget or the calendar. Or you have a script and a deadline and nothing else. Food and drink, D2C, jewellery, watches and SaaS brands are most of what we do.

Not for you if the film needs a camera crew on location, a presenter, or real people tasting the product. We build in motion; a shoot is a different service and we will say so on the first call.

What we make

Hyper-motion product films: ten to thirty seconds, the product as the hero, built in 3D and motion from your product photos or files. Motion-graphics ads for feeds and screens. 2D explainers for products that need a story told in order. 3D product visualisation and still packs for listings and launches. Every film is delivered in the formats you run: 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, with and without text.

How it is made

A one-page direction first: the idea, the references and a timestamped beat sheet you approve before anything is animated. Then the film is built in-house, so the first draft is the real thing. Sound is designed with the picture. Revision rounds are agreed up front and feedback goes in one list.

Timeline

Standard turnaround is 7 to 10 business days from an approved direction. The QCSTech Convo AI launch film went from script to screen in one week. Rush delivery in four business days or less is possible at plus 50 percent.

How a film runs

Short, in writing, and the same every time.

  1. BriefSend the product, the goal, the deadline and anything you have: photos, a script, a 3D file. We reply with an approach and a fixed quote.
  2. DirectionOne page: the idea, references and a beat sheet with timestamps. You approve it before animation starts.
  3. BuildThe film is built in-house in 3D and motion, with sound designed to the picture.
  4. RefineOne revision round in Standard, two in Premium. Feedback in one list, changes back as one version.
  5. DeliverMaster files in every format you run, with and without text, plus cutdowns if they were in the brief.

Questions we get

Straight answers, the same ones we give on the first call.

How much does a product film cost in India?

Our motion-graphics ads start at INR 12,000 for 15 seconds and hyper-motion 3D product films at INR 20,000 for 15 seconds, excluding GST. Published Indian market guides put 3D product animation at INR 75,000 to 1.7 lakh per minute, so a 15-second film from us sits well under the market rate for 3D. The full breakdown is on our product film cost page.

Do I need a shoot or product photos?

No shoot. We build the product in 3D from clear product photos, pack artwork or a 3D file if you have one. If the product has to be modelled from scratch, that is INR 5,000 to 15,000 depending on complexity, and we tell you before quoting.

How long does it take?

Standard turnaround is 7 to 10 business days from an approved direction. Rush delivery in four business days or less is plus 50 percent. Tell us the real deadline on day one and we plan backwards from it.

What formats do I get?

Whatever you run: 9:16 for reels and stories, 1:1 for feeds, 16:9 for screens and YouTube, each with and without text. Extra ratios after delivery are plus 10 percent per format, so list every placement in the brief.

Can you work from a script we already have?

Yes. QCSTech and QED Vault both sent finished scripts and we designed, animated and scored the films from them. If there is no script, the direction page covers the story.

Where can the film run?

Organic and paid digital for 12 months in one market is included. Global or perpetual digital use is plus 20 percent. Television, outdoor and print at scale are quoted separately.
