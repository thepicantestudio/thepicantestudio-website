Using it well

Honestly, Vaibhav

Give it room

Keep empty space around the logo at least as wide as the doorway. Nothing else inside the dashed line.

Minimum sizes

  • Full logo: 140 px wide on screen, 35 mm in print.
  • Mark on its own: 24 px.
  • Below that, use the mark without the wordmark.

Please don’t

  • Stretch, rotate or add shadows.
  • Recolour it outside the palette below.
  • Put it straight on a busy photo. Use a paper or ink panel.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font.

Voice

How Honestly, Vaibhav sounds, in posts, captions and on the website.

Say it straight

No sugarcoating and no jargon. If it’s a myth, call it a myth, then explain why.

Warm, not soft

Direct doesn’t mean cold. Start with understanding, then say the true thing.

Talk the way people think

English, Hindi or Hinglish, mixed naturally. “Therapy sirf ‘serious’ problems ke liye nahi hoti.”

Backed by evidence

Only what research supports. No controversy for reach, and never a client’s story.

Sounds like us

  • “Here’s what therapy actually is.”
  • “You don’t need the right words to start.”
  • “Perspectives, not advice.”
  • Ending with “Honestly, Vaibhav”.

Not us

  • “Fix your life in 5 steps.”
  • Diagnosing people or calling them “toxic” online.
  • Words like “crazy” or “weak”, even as jokes.
  • Clickbait, before-and-after promises, testimonials.
