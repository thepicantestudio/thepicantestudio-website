About

I’m Vaibhav. I’m real in the room.

I’m a psychologist in Delhi NCR. I work with adults who are tired of coping quietly, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, online and in person.

Why I do this

A lot of us grew up having our feelings dismissed.

In India especially, people still hesitate to come to therapy. Stigma, fear of judgment, family pressure, or simply not knowing what it is.

Many people believe therapy is only for “serious mental illness”, or for people who are weak. So they grow up suppressing emotions, avoiding conversations about how they really are, and treating emotional suffering as normal. Not because they want to, but because for most of their lives, what they felt was brushed aside.

I became a psychologist to be the opposite of that. A place where what you feel is taken seriously, looked at honestly, and understood.

I don’t sugarcoat things, and I don’t hand out advice. I take time to explain what is happening in your mind when that helps, and I let trust build at its own pace. My approach is eclectic: I use what fits you, not one method for everyone.

How I am in the room

Gentle, but honest.

Techniques matter. But what people remember is how it felt to be in the conversation.

  1. Human-centred and conversational
  2. Emotionally present, not purely technique-driven
  3. Exploration over judgment
  4. Meaning and identity, beyond reducing symptoms
  5. Psychology, expressive arts and creativity, together
  6. Gentle but honest conversations
  7. From surviving, towards a more meaningful way of living

Honestly

Am I the right therapist for you?

Probably yes, if…

  • You have been wanting a safe space to think out loud
  • You want perspective, not advice
  • You know this is different from talking to friends or family
  • You are willing to show up and do the work, at your own pace

Probably not, if…

  • You are under 17. I only work with people 17 and above
  • You want someone to solve your life for you, or a quick fix
  • You need to be convinced that therapy is worth trying
  • You are in crisis right now. Please call Tele-MANAS on 14416
