Services

Four ways to work together.

Online from anywhere, or in person in Delhi NCR. In English, Hindi or Hinglish. For anyone aged 17 and above.

Adults, one to one

This is where most people start. A private, regular conversation that is about you and nobody else.

People come with anxiety and overthinking, burnout, low self-worth, shame, relationship patterns, big life changes, or a feeling of being stuck that they can’t quite name. You don’t need a diagnosis, and your problem does not need to be “serious enough”.

Message Vaibhav
  • Sessions of about 50 minutes
  • Online, or in person in Delhi NCR
  • Usually weekly to begin with
  • For anyone aged 17 and above
Couples

For two people who want to understand what keeps happening between them. The same argument on repeat, distance that has crept in, or a decision you can’t make together.

I don’t take sides and I don’t decide who is right. I help you both hear what the other one is actually saying.

Message Vaibhav
  • Both partners attend together
  • Online, or in person in Delhi NCR
  • Married, unmarried, any orientation
Groups

Small, guided circles built around one shared theme, often using expressive arts. Hearing someone else say the thing you thought only you felt is powerful in a way one-to-one work can’t be.

Groups open a few times a year. The waitlist hears about them first.

Join the waitlist
  • Small groups, guided by me throughout
  • Online and in person in Delhi NCR
  • A short chat first, to check the group is right for you
Companies and colleges

Talks, workshops and ongoing support for teams and students. Stress and burnout, emotional awareness, how to support a colleague or friend, and how to talk about mental health without the clichés.

Everything is built around your people and your context. Tell me a little about them and I’ll come back with a proposal.

What people bring

You don’t need the right words.

These are the things I work with most, in the words people usually use for them.

  • Anxiety and chronic worry
  • Overthinking and rumination
  • Emotional overwhelm
  • Fear and not feeling safe
  • Chronic stress and burnout
  • Feeling hopeless or stuck
  • Low self-worth and self-criticism
  • Shame and guilt
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Identity struggles
  • Major life transitions
  • Exhaustion and stress in the body
  • Health-related fears
  • Meaning, purpose and direction
  • Surviving rather than living
  • Reconnecting with joy and creativity
  • Personality-related difficulties, including experience with BPD
