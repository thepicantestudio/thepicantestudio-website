How I work

Not one method. The one that fits you.

Therapy is not one size fits all. I’m present in the conversation first, and I bring in a method when it helps. These are the six I draw on most.

Expressive arts therapy

Some things don’t come out in sentences. We might draw, write, use music or movement to reach what words keep missing.

You don’t need to be artistic. It is about expression, not skill, and you never have to show anyone what you make.

Feeling stuckEmotional overwhelmReconnecting with joy and creativity

Existential and meaning-oriented work

Sometimes the question isn’t “what is wrong with me?” but “what is the point?”. We look at your values, the choices in front of you, and who you are outside the roles you play.

The aim is more than fewer symptoms. It is a life that feels like yours.

Meaning and directionIdentityMajor life transitions

Emotion-focused work

Many of us were taught to push feelings down. Here we slow down, name what is actually there, and let it make sense.

A feeling that has been understood stops running the show from the background.

Shame and guiltRelationshipsEmotional processing

CBT and DBT skills

Structured, practical and well researched. We map the loop between your thoughts, feelings and actions, and find where it can be interrupted.

Then we practise: skills for anxious spirals, for intense emotions, and for the conversations that usually go wrong.

Anxiety and chronic worryOverthinkingEmotional dysregulation

Mindfulness and mind–body awareness

Stress lives in the body: the tight chest, the racing heart, the tiredness that sleep doesn’t fix. We practise noticing those signals early.

With time you learn to settle your body, instead of being carried along by it.

BurnoutChronic fatigueHealth-related anxiety

Self-compassion and self-awareness

If the voice in your head is a harsh critic, everything is harder. We work on seeing yourself clearly, and on speaking to yourself the way you would to someone you care about.

This is not about letting yourself off the hook. People who are kinder to themselves tend to change more, not less.

Low self-worthSelf-criticismPersonal growth

What a first session looks like

Four small steps. No surprises.

Not knowing what happens is what stops most people. So here is exactly what happens.

Keep scrolling

01

You send a message.

Use the form on this site, or WhatsApp me. Say as much or as little as you like. I read and reply to every message myself.

02

We have a short chat.

A few messages or a quick call. You ask your questions, I understand what you are looking for, and we both see whether I’m the right fit. I share my fees here, with no obligation.

03

The first session.

Fifty minutes, online or in Delhi NCR. No tests, no lying on a couch. You talk, I listen and ask questions, and we start making sense of things together.

04

What happens next.

We agree on what you want from therapy and how often to meet. Most people start weekly. You are never locked in, and you can stop whenever you want.
