Most people who come to me are not broken. They are tired. Tired of overthinking, of holding it together, of being told to “just stay positive”. They want to move from surviving to actually living.

How I work

Not one method. The one that fits you.

I’m present in the conversation rather than driven by technique. Underneath that, I draw on six ways of working, and we use what helps.

  • Expressive arts

    Drawing, music, movement and writing, for the things that are hard to say in words.

  • Existential and meaning-oriented

    Values, purpose and identity. Who you are beyond the symptoms.

  • Emotion-focused

    Making room for what you feel, so it can be understood instead of pushed down.

  • CBT and DBT skills

    Practical tools for anxious thoughts, big emotions and the moments that usually go wrong.

  • Mindfulness and mind–body

    Noticing what your body is already telling you, and learning to slow it down.

  • Self-compassion and self-awareness

    Changing how you speak to yourself, which changes more than you would expect.

What a first session looks like

Four small steps. No surprises.

Not knowing what happens is what stops most people. So here is exactly what happens.

Keep scrolling

01

You send a message.

Use the form on this site, or WhatsApp me. Say as much or as little as you like. I read and reply to every message myself.

02

We have a short chat.

A few messages or a quick call. You ask your questions, I understand what you are looking for, and we both see whether I’m the right fit. I share my fees here, with no obligation.

03

The first session.

Fifty minutes, online or in Delhi NCR. No tests, no lying on a couch. You talk, I listen and ask questions, and we start making sense of things together.

04

What happens next.

We agree on what you want from therapy and how often to meet. Most people start weekly. You are never locked in, and you can stop whenever you want.

A note from me

About Vaibhav

A lot of us grew up having our feelings dismissed.

So we learnt to keep quiet, to call it “just stress”, to believe therapy is only for people who are weak or seriously unwell. It isn’t. It is a room where you are taken seriously.

I’m a psychologist with a master’s in clinical psychology, trained in expressive arts therapy and clinical hypnotherapy. I see clients at Fortis Hospital, Noida, in private clinics across Delhi NCR, and online across India and abroad. I’m real in my sessions. I take time to explain what is happening in your mind, and I let trust build at its own pace.
