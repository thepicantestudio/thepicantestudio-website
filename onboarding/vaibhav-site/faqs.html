FAQs

The questions people are afraid to ask.

Honest answers. If yours isn’t here, message me and ask. There are no silly questions about this.

Before you start

Doubts are normal.

Does therapy actually work?

For most people, yes, and there is a lot of research behind that. But it is not a magic potion. It works when you show up regularly, are honest, and are willing to look at things that are uncomfortable. My job is to make that feel safe enough to do.

Is my problem serious enough for therapy?

If it is bothering you, it is serious enough. Therapy is not only for crisis or for “serious mental illness”. You don’t have to wait until things fall apart. Many people come simply because they are tired of feeling the way they feel.

How do I know if you are the right therapist for me?

You won’t know until we talk, which is why we start with a short chat before any session. Notice whether you feel listened to and whether you could be honest with me. If I’m not the right fit, I will tell you, and help you find someone who is.

Will you tell me what to do?

No. A therapist is not a problem-solver for your life. I won’t give you advice the way friends and family do. I’ll help you see your situation more clearly, so the decisions you make are really yours.

Is what I say confidential? What will people think?

What you tell me stays with me. Nobody, including your family or employer, is told that you see me or what we talk about. The only exception is a serious and immediate risk to your life or someone else’s, and I would talk to you first. Online sessions mean nobody even has to see you walk into a clinic.

What does it cost?

I share my fees in our first message or chat, before you commit to anything. There is no obligation and no sales talk. Fees are different for clients in India and clients abroad.

I don’t have time. How much does it take?

About 50 minutes a session, usually once a week to begin with. Online sessions remove the travel, so many people fit it into a lunch break or an evening. We can space sessions out once things feel steadier.

What happens in the first session?

No tests and no lying on a couch. You tell me what brought you here, in whatever order it comes out. I listen, ask questions, and explain how I think I can help. By the end we agree on what you want from therapy and how often to meet. See the four steps.

Online or in person: which is better?

Both work well. Online suits people outside Delhi NCR, people abroad, and anyone who wants privacy or has little time. In person suits people who find it easier to open up in a room. You can switch between them.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Hindi and Hinglish. Most people end up using a mix, and that is completely fine. Say it in whichever language it comes out.

Do you work with children or teenagers?

I work with people aged 17 and above. For younger children I’m happy to suggest colleagues who specialise in child psychology.

What is clinical hypnotherapy? Is it like on TV?

Nothing like TV. You stay aware and in control the whole time. It is a focused, relaxed state that can help with certain habits and anxieties. I’m trained in it, and I only suggest it when it is appropriate and you are comfortable. Most of my work doesn’t involve it.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Life happens. Tell me as early as you can, ideally a day before, and we will find another time. We go over the details before your first session so there are no surprises.

Can you prescribe medication?

No. I’m a psychologist, not a psychiatrist, so I don’t prescribe. If I think medication might help alongside therapy, I’ll say so honestly and can refer you to a psychiatrist.
