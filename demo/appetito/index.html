Pick your afternoon

Play first, or eat first?

Everything is a two-minute walk apart. Send the kids to the green and keep the table, or earn your lunch on the track.

01 · The restaurant

Dim sum, wood-fired pizza, dal makhani.

Fifteen pages that run from Thai Highway Rolls and jhol dim sum to fresh-dough pizza, risotto and butter chicken, under the rattan lamps or out in the courtyard. Mocktails, shakes and coffee all day.

  • Indoors + courtyard
  • Veg and non-veg
  • 11 am to 11 pm

02 · Mini golf

Eighteen holes. Zero handicap.

A landscaped course with bunkers, boulders and water, big enough for a birthday party and small enough for a five-year-old. After the food, it is the thing reviewers mention first.

  • 18 holes
  • From ₹400 a round
  • 11 am to 9 pm
Book a round

03 · Pump track

Pump it. Never pedal it.

Twelve thousand square feet of dirt rollers and berms. You ride the hills, not the pedals, and the track teaches you how. Walk-ins all day, training programmes for kids who have just lost their stabilisers.

  • Walk-ins welcome
  • From ₹300 a session
  • Training programmes
Book a session

04 · Pickleball

Two courts. Floodlit till late.

Paddles and balls on hire, a cold drink thirty steps away. The courts hosted an open DUPR-rated championship in May 2026, and the next one is on the calendar.

  • 2 courts, floodlit
  • From ₹600 an hour
  • Equipment on hire
Book a court

Groups

Birthdays, offsites, tournament nights.

Ten minutes from Expo Mart and the Knowledge Park campuses. Tell us the date and the headcount and we plan the day: a round of golf, a court, a long table, a cake.

Birthday on the green

Mini golf for the kids, a long table under the lamps for the parents, a cake from the kitchen. Weekday afternoons are the quiet ones.

Team day out

Pickleball doubles, a pump track race, a working lunch. Corporate groups from Knowledge Park and Noida are twenty minutes away by car.

Tournaments

The courts hosted an open DUPR-rated pickleball championship in May 2026. Leagues, ladders and club nights are next. Ask to be on the list.
