Pick your afternoon

Lunch first, or the game first?

Everything is a short walk apart. Play, then eat. Eat, then play. Or send the kids to the green and keep the table.

01 · The restaurant

Thai rolls, wood-fired pizza, butter chicken.

Rattan lamps, cane chairs and a courtyard under the trees. The menu runs from dim sum and pad kee mao to fresh-dough pizza, risotto and a proper dal makhani, with mocktails, shakes and coffee all day.

  • Indoors + courtyard
  • Veg and non-veg
  • 11 am to 11 pm

02 · Mini golf

Eighteen holes, no handicap needed.

A landscaped course with bunkers, boulders and water, big enough for a birthday party and small enough for a five-year-old. The first thing people mention in reviews after the food.

  • 18 holes
  • From ₹400 a round

03 · Pump track

Rollers, berms, and no pedalling.

A 12,000 square foot dirt track built for pumping: you ride the hills, not the pedals. Walk-ins, bike rental and training programmes for kids who have just lost their stabilisers.

  • Walk-ins welcome
  • From ₹300 a session
  • Bikes on hire
Book a session

04 · Pickleball

Paddle. Play. Repeat.

Two floodlit courts, paddles and balls on hire, a cold drink thirty steps away. Appetito ran its first open DUPR-rated championship in May 2026, and the next one is coming.

  • 2 courts, floodlit
  • From ₹600 an hour
  • Equipment on hire

Groups

Birthdays, offsites, tournament nights.

Ten minutes from Expo Mart and the Knowledge Park campuses. Tell us the date and the headcount and we plan the day: a round of golf, a court, a table, a cake.

Birthday on the green

Mini golf for the kids, a long table under the lamps for the parents, a cake from the kitchen. Weekday afternoons are the quiet ones.

Team day out

Pickleball doubles, a pump track race, a working lunch. Projector on request. Corporate groups from Knowledge Park and Noida come by car in twenty minutes.

Tournaments

The courts hosted an open DUPR-rated pickleball championship in May 2026. Leagues, ladders and club nights are next. Ask to be on the list.
