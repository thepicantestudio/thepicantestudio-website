Finding us

It's worth the walk.

Maps give up on the last stretch, so the crew drew their own. Park at Dona Paula Circle, then follow it on foot, in shoes you can walk in.

  3. 3On your left, follow the cobbled stone path through the village, past a small white cross on your right.

Before you set sail

Asked across the bar.

My map app gives up near the jetty. Am I lost?

No, GPS just has no sea legs. Cars stop at the main entrance, then it's a short walk along the shore following the markers, in shoes you can walk in.

Can I just walk in, or do I need to send word ahead?

Weekends and sunsets fill up, so a WhatsApp to +91 91468 67585 holds your seat. And not on a Tuesday, when the ship rests.

Over a hundred rums. Where does a beginner start?

Ask for a flight and let the crew walk you from light and grassy to dark and funky, with a story behind every pour. The Horde was gathered on the crew's travels and topped up by visitors who left a bottle behind.

Who's behind the bar, and will they laugh at me?

Arijit Bose and Francis Coelho are the captains, and every bartender here pours with a story. Tell them you know nothing about rum and they'll take it as an invitation.

Will my wallet survive?

The cocktails cost more than the Goa average, and nobody pretends otherwise. You're paying for the rum, the room and the sea, and the one with fire on top earns its keep.

When's the show?

Sunset, through the bay windows, with the waves doing the sound. Doors at 3 pm on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday, noon Friday to Sunday, shut on Tuesday.

Is it true people sing Titanic in here?

Some nights, late, once the regulars decide the moment has come. We don't promise it, and we don't stop it.

What if I'm the one not drinking tonight?

Then you eat. Fresh seafood and Goan home cooking, built to sit beside the rum, and if you're vegetarian, say so when you book so the galley can plan.
