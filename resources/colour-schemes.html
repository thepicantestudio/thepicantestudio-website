50 three-colour schemes

Fifty palettes of a ground, a voice and an accent, each with hex codes and the kind of brand it suits.

By Raunak Singh Bhogal. Published 18 September 2026, updated 26 September 2026.

The short version

Three colours is enough for a brand: one ground, one voice, one accent. Seven schemes with hex codes below, ten more after them, and all fifty in the PDF, with the 60, 30, 10 rule for using them with discipline.

The 60, 30, 10 rule

The ground covers about 60 percent of any surface, the voice about 30, the accent about 10. Backgrounds and large areas take the ground. Type and structure take the voice. Buttons, highlights and one detail take the accent. Break the ratio on purpose, never by accident.
