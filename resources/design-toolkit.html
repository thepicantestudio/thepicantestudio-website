The design toolkit

Twenty-six sites and tools for inspiration, fonts, music, motion, 3D and AI. Checked live, no affiliate links.

By Raunak Singh Bhogal. Published 18 September 2026, updated 26 September 2026.

The short version

Twenty-six sites and tools we actually open: for inspiration, fonts, music, motion, 3D and AI. Checked live, no affiliate links. The full list is below, grouped the way we use it.

7 sites designers open before they open Figma.

Stuck on a layout? The answer is already live somewhere. These are where to look.

Awwwards awwwards.com

The awards site for web design. Site of the Day is a free daily lesson in what the best studios are shipping.

Best for: seeing where the bar is this month.

Recent recent.design

A fast, endless wall of the newest well-designed sites. Formerly Godly. No articles, just the work.

Best for: a ten-minute scroll before you start.

Minimal Gallery minimal.gallery

Hand-picked sites where less is doing more. Great when a client says clean and means it.

Best for: type-led, quiet layouts.

Httpster httpster.net

A long-running gallery of sites with personality. Filter by type and style.

Best for: portfolios and studio sites.

Siteinspire siteinspire.com

A deep archive you can filter by style, type and subject. Old enough to show what ages well.

Best for: finding a reference in a niche.

Land-book land-book.com

Landing pages, product pages and shops, sorted and searchable.

Best for: anything that has to convert.

Navbar and Footer galleries navbar.gallery + footer.design

Two tiny galleries for the two parts of a site everyone rushes. One for navigation, one for footers.

Best for: the last 10% of a build.

6 AI tools worth a designer's time.

Most AI tools are a demo. These six are in real workflows. One warning at the end.

Flair flair.ai

Drop in a product photo and build a studio scene around it. Made for e-commerce images.

Use it for: quick product scenes and mock shoots.

Spline spline.design

3D design in the browser with real-time collaboration. Model, animate and embed it on a site.

Use it for: 3D hero sections without Blender.

Neurons neuronsinc.com

Predicts where eyes land on an ad or layout before you spend money running it.

Use it for: testing a key visual before launch.

Recraft recraft.ai

Image generation that also outputs vectors, custom styles and mockups.

Use it for: icons and illustrations in one style.

Relume relume.io

Sitemaps and wireframes for marketing sites, generated from a prompt and editable.

Use it for: the first hour of a web project.

10Web 10web.io

AI website building, hosting and optimisation on WordPress.

Use it for: a fast first version for a small business.

The warning taste is not included

None of these pick the idea, the palette or the typeface for you. They make a designer faster. They do not make one.

Tools multiply taste. Zero times anything is zero.

You are not bad at design. You just do not know these sites.

Seven free places that fix the usual problems: fonts, music, heroes, motion and references.

Best Free Fonts bestfreefonts.com

A curated shelf of free typefaces that do not look free, sorted for branding, web and editorial.

Fixes: the default-font look.

Supahero supahero.io

A hand-picked library of website hero sections. Nothing else, just the first screen.

Fixes: a weak first impression.

Cosmos cosmos.so

A calm place to collect references. Like a moodboard app with no ads and no noise.

Fixes: references lost in screenshots.

Savee savee.it

Visual references chosen by designers. Collect what matters and train your eye.

Fixes: not knowing what good looks like.

Jitter jitter.video

Motion design in the browser. Animate a layout in minutes with no After Effects.

Fixes: static posts in a moving feed.

Uppbeat uppbeat.io

Music for creators with a free tier. Tracks that will not get your reel muted.

Fixes: copyright strikes.

Mobbin mobbin.com

Hundreds of thousands of real app and web screens, searchable by flow and pattern.

Fixes: guessing how an interface should work.
