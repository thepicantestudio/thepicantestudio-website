The listing image checklist

Seven images in the order a buyer needs them, for Flipkart, Amazon, Blinkit, Zepto and your own store.

By Raunak Singh Bhogal. Published 18 September 2026, updated 26 September 2026.

The short version

Seven images in the order a buyer needs them, for Flipkart, Amazon, Blinkit, Zepto and your own store. Check each marketplace's current size and background rules before you upload.

The hero

The pack, front on, on a clean white ground, filling the frame. It is the only image most people ever see, so it has to read at thumbnail size.

Test it at 100 pixels wide. If the name vanishes, fix the crop.

The scale shot

In a hand, in a bag, next to a glass. Online, nobody knows if it is 50 ml or 500 ml until you show them.

A wrong-size surprise is a return waiting to happen.

The one benefit

One line of big type on the image. The single reason to buy, not five. People read an image before they read the title.

Six words or fewer.

What is inside

Ingredients, specs or contents as a clean graphic. This is where the careful buyer decides.

Group into three or four facts. Never a wall of text.

In use

The product doing its job in a real setting. This image sells the feeling, the others sell the facts.

One setting that matches your buyer, not a stock kitchen.

The proof

Lab tested, certified, made in India, rated 4.6. Whatever you can honestly claim, as badges on one image.

Only claims you can back. Marketplaces can remove listings for false ones.

The range

The other flavours, sizes or colours. The last image should sell the second product.

Same layout for every product, so the range looks like a family.

The seven images

  • Hero: pack front, clean white ground, fills the frame
  • Scale: in hand or beside a familiar object
  • One benefit: a single line, six words or fewer
  • What is inside: three or four facts as a graphic
  • In use: one real setting that fits your buyer
  • Proof: only claims you can back, as badges
  • The range: other variants, same layout

Before you upload

  • Hero still reads at 100 pixels wide
  • Text on images is large enough for a phone
  • Same background, light and angle across the whole range
  • Square versions exported, highest resolution the marketplace allows
  • A short looping film ready for the video slot, if the marketplace has one

The fast way

Build one set as a template. Every new flavour or colour then takes a day, not a shoot. That is a visual system, and it is what we build.
