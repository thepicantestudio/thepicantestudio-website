Privacy

Short, because what we do with your details is simple.

What we collect

When you download a resource we ask for your name and email. A phone number and a company name are optional. We also see the country the request came from. This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no trackers.

Why

To send you what you asked for, and to contact you about it if it looks like we can help. We only do that if you tick the consent box.

Where it goes

The form sends your details to the studio's own inbox. They are not sold, rented or shared with anyone outside Picante Studio. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes the request on our behalf.

Your choice

Email hello@thepicantestudio.com at any time to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We act on it promptly.
