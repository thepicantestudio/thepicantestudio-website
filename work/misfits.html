Misfits

A concept launch film for a prebiotic soda: one continuous pull-back from a droplet to the can.

The business

Misfits is a Thane-based prebiotic soda startup with illustrated cans and strong CGI stills of its own.

The brief we set

The brand's imagery is still. A category built for reels had no motion. Could a launch film match the brand's own bar and add what it lacked?

The idea

One mechanic, the scale lie: open on an extreme macro of a droplet and a chilli on a golden landscape, then pull back continuously until the landscape is the can. Four headlines, one accent word each. The real can artwork carried through the whole move.

How it was made

Prompt-directed generation from a Picante-written beat sheet, with the real can as the reference and the copy list as a contract. Sound designed to the beats, finished in post.

What it shows

That a brand with a high visual bar can get motion at the same level without a shoot, and that the artwork it already owns survives the move intact.
