The business

Bangalore Watch Company is an Indian mechanical-watch brand and the first Indian independent at Geneva Watch Days 2026. The Peninsula Carbon is a 100-piece limited edition with a recovered-carbon dial.

The brief we set

A watch this technical is usually shown on a wrist. Could a ten-second film show the inside?

The idea

The strap is the timeline. One continuous take on black: the watch decomposes along a single vertical axis into an exploded stack, the camera flies between the layers, four facts land as tracked callouts, and the stack slams back together.

How it was made

A locked start frame generated from the product photo, then a single-take film directed by a timestamped beat sheet. Callouts were limited to facts that can be verified.

What it shows

Premium product film without a macro rig or a teardown: the engineering as the story, for brands whose value is inside the case.
