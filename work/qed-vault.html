QED Vault

A 97-second 4K marketing film for a product-authentication platform: script rewritten, designed, edited and scored by Picante.

Client work
Client
QED Vault
Service
Create: motion film for SaaS
Delivered
One 4K film, 97 seconds, 16:9, with voice-over, music and sound design
How it came in
Referred by QCSTech

The business

QED Vault is a product-authentication platform. It authenticates products and turns every token movement into real-time data, a detailed view of a product's journey, so the data becomes a strategic asset rather than a by-product.

The problem

QED Vault came to Picante on QCSTech's referral, with a written script and nothing else. The product lives in tokens and journeys rather than screens, and the film had to explain authentication and data provenance in one sitting, clearly enough to pitch with.

The idea

Treat the journey as the set: the product's path from release into the world, through the channels it travels, rendered as motion in the client's palette. Dark circuit-world beats for the token layer, clean light beats for the human moments.

How it was made

It started with a long meeting to understand exactly what the team needed the film to do. Then the script was rewritten: shorter, punchier, in the rhythm of a SaaS film.

From that meeting and the client's visual references, Picante built a full draft with the voice-over in place. One feedback meeting on the draft, one revision to the client's notes, then sound effects and music, and delivery of the finished film.

Outcome

The film now does the explaining. QED Vault uses it to show what the product is and to pitch it to its own clients.
