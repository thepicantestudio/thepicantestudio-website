The business

Jimmy's Cocktails is an Indian ready-to-drink mixer brand with fully colour-coated bottles, one colour per flavour, sold direct and through bars.

The brief we set

A flavour range this colourful deserves a motion system, not a photo. Could one film and one still library serve social, ads and a bar-trade pitch at once?

The idea

Flood by flavour: the world takes the bottle's colour, then the chilli changes it. Mango floods the floor, a chilli lands, a red pulse climbs the bottle, the pour lands in a highball, the lockup lands on cobalt.

Around it, a still library in the same colour-block language: studio bases per flavour, conceptual pieces, lifestyle scenes, a five-slide carousel and an ad pack.

How it was made

Prompt-directed generation from Picante beat sheets and a locked visual system, with the real bottles as references. One re-render to fix type placement, then finished in post.

What it shows

What a month of content for a drinks brand could look like when the film, the stills and the ads come out of one system. It is the base for Picante's Scale demo month.
