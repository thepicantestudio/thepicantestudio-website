The brief

Say what the studio does in ten seconds, to someone scrolling, without a word of agency language.

The idea

A flat vector world and one mechanic: a grey product nobody wants, then the chili dot lands on it, and every hard colour switch after that is one of the three jobs. A film on chili, a site on cobalt, a month of content on lime. The dot is the agent of change; the copy is the pitch.

How it was made

The start frame was generated flat, then a single 10-second generation with foley and a percussion bed. The model gave the character a face and set the lockup on bone inside a chili circle. Both were kept.

Where it runs

Lead film on the website and the first reel of the studio's launch month.
