About

A creative production, design and content studio. Premium creative, without the premium-agency barrier.

Mission

Make every business look as good as the work behind it.

Good products lose on presentation every day. The premium look sits behind premium-agency pricing, and everything below it looks like a template. Picante exists to close that gap: studio-level taste and craft, at a price a growing brand can say yes to.

Creative is not decoration. It is how a business communicates quality, professionalism and value, and how it justifies its price. So every film, system and site we make has one job: make the client's business look better, communicate better and, where it matters, sell better.

The long game is to be a creative production partner, not a design vendor. Take an idea from concept, to visual execution, to ongoing production, with one point of view running through all of it.

Founder and CEO

Raunak Singh Bhogal

Raunak has spent five years in the creative industry, three of them as a founder, doing the jobs most studios split across a department: creative direction, design, video and film-making, motion graphics, web design and build, and music. He directs the idea, then makes it himself.

That range is the whole point of Picante. A launch film, its sound, the site it lands on and the month of content that follows can come from one head and one pair of hands, so nothing is lost in a handover and nothing waits on a third party. It is also why the studio moves at the speed it does: brief to finished 4K film in a week, brief to live site in days.

He is an expert in each of these fields rather than a generalist who dabbles, and he holds the work to the same bar in all of them: taste first, then quality, then commercial value, then what production can realistically deliver. Nothing is recommended because it is trendy, cheap or merely impressive.

He founded Picante in 2025 on a simple observation from those years: the businesses with the best products were rarely the ones that looked the best. Picante is his answer, a studio built to give any product, service or brand a premium feel, and to make people want it.
